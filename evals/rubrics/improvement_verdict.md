Rubric for the verdict session of the weekly improvement review (session `review_improvement.verdict`): a proposal to keep, adjust or drop one improvement, with a reason and a next experiment.

Score the output from 1 to 5:

- **5** – The verdict follows from the evidence (keep: it works or too early to tell; adjust: partly works or hits an obstacle; drop: no benefit or abandoned); the reason cites what the quotes show without adding facts; the next experiment is one concrete, small, sensible step.
- **4** – Right verdict and a faithful reason, with a minor weakness: a slightly vague next experiment, or a reason that does not mention the strongest quote.
- **3** – Defensible verdict but a thin reason, a generic next experiment, or a small claim not in the evidence.
- **2** – The verdict does not fit the evidence, or the reason invents facts.
- **1** – Wrong, contradicts the evidence, or empty.

Judge only against the evidence in the input; the agent only proposes, the owner decides.
